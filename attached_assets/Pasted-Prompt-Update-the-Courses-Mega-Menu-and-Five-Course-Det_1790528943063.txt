4. Reuse Faculty records for mentors
Do not create separate mentor records for Courses. Extend the existing Faculty profiles managed in the Faculty admin area:

Add a multi-select Teaching Subjects field, separate from any existing profile subject/specialization field.

Show a faculty member in a course’s Meet Your Mentors section only if:

Show on Courses page is enabled, and
Their teaching subjects match that course.

Use the corresponding subject mapping for BiPC and each related course. Show each mentor only once even if they match more than one subject. Keep course visibility independent of whether the Faculty page or Home preview toggles are enabled.
