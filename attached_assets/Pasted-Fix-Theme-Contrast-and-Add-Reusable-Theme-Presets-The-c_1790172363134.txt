Required contrast behavior
On light backgrounds:

Main text should use a dark readable color.
Headings and labels should use the primary theme color or a dark neutral.
Accent red should be used for highlights, borders, dots, icons, and active details.
Do not use white text on pale accent backgrounds.
Do not reduce text opacity so much that it becomes unreadable.
On dark backgrounds:

Use white or a sufficiently light foreground color.
Preserve readable muted text.
Use accent colors only where contrast remains accessible.
Do not rely on accidental inherited text colors.
