Separate Alumni Success Stories from Results Student Records
The Success Stories section is for alumni—former students who have passed out. It is separate from the current students listed in Top Performers and the Complete Merit List.

Remove the incorrect student-record fields
In the Results admin editor, remove these fields from individual student result records:

Student success story
Publish this success story
Story carousel order
Remove the rule that generates the Success Stories carousel from published Results student records. The Success Stories section must not use current Top Performer or merit-list students as its content source.

Keep each student’s result information—such as name, photo, exam, rank, marks, course, and year—intact. If the existing performer dialog remains, keep it focused on achievement details; do not show alumni testimonials there or link it to the alumni story records.

Add separate alumni stories in Results admin
In Pages → Results, add a dedicated Alumni Success Stories management section directly after Results Statistics. Do not nest these records inside student result cards.

Let administrators add, edit, reorder, publish/unpublish, and delete alumni stories. Each story can include:

Alumni display name
Optional portrait and alt text
Passing year or batch
Course or stream
Current college, career, or outcome, if available
Testimonial or story text
Publish status
Carousel display order
Results page carousel
Use the separately managed alumni stories in the Results-page Success Stories section. Keep the three-card auto-scrolling presentation and its animation. Remove the View All Stories button; do not replace it with a link to the Results page.

Do not invent alumni or testimonials. If fewer than three stories are published, show only the available ones.

Preserve any entered content
If story text has already been saved in student result records, preserve it rather than silently deleting it. Move it to a separate unpublished alumni-story draft for administrator review; do not automatically label current Results students as alumni or publish those stories.

Keep the CTA buttons linked to Contact, reuse the Home page’s shared footer, and preserve the existing shared Results library and Home preview. Keep Results management under Pages → Results.

Verify that alumni stories can be edited independently, current Results students no longer supply carousel stories, and existing result records remain intact.
